Every nation's strength lies in its people.

At its heart, this initiative is an inspiration for transformation - a catalyst for shaping the future of Sri Lanka through the collective strength of its people.

It is a movement to recognize bold leadership, commitment to community development, and their ability to fuel change that extends far beyond the workplace.

Purpose

Identify & celebrate organizations whose contributions uplift the nation.

Highlight impactful work that drives economic growth and community development.

Encourage emulation by inspiring businesses across Sri Lanka to follow these examples, fostering collective progress and stability.

Inspire a vision for Sri Lanka's future as a thriving hub in South Asia, anchored by its talent pool.

Showcase achievements that spark a national movement.

Creating lasting progress – focus on generational impact for Sri Lanka's people and communities.

Benefits

Position your organization as a national icon—earning validation as a driver of Sri Lanka's progress.

Be recognized as a trusted leader, showcasing tangible impact through social responsibility and inclusive growth—attracting partnerships, and opportunities.

Gain strategic business advantage by building investor confidence and standing out in competitive markets.

Improve customer loyalty through stronger emotional connection and shared purpose with an organization that recognizes and champions nation builders.

Attract top talent while elevating employee trust, pride, retention, and advocacy.

About Us

Vision

A Sri Lanka recognized as a thriving hub in South Asia, anchored by the strength and talent of its people.

Mission

To identify and celebrate organizations whose contributions uplift the nation, inspiring collective progress across Sri Lankan industry.

How we assess impact

The Nation Builders recognition framework evaluates organizations through three pillars: People, Planet and Profit, with the strongest emphasis on workplace culture, employee experience, and measurable national impact.

What shapes the score

  • Employee feedback and experience indicators
  • Culture Audit and people practices
  • Community, social and environmental contribution
  • Economic impact, governance and long-term business sustainability
